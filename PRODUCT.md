# Avela Travel

## Register
brand

## Product
Marketing site for Avela Travel, a luxury travel business in Nosara, Costa Rica. Avela plans full trips: hand-picked villas, private chefs, surf, sportfishing, wellness, and ground transport. Sister company is Monkey Head Adventures (boats). The site's job is to communicate a specific, anti-resort point of view and convert visitors into trip inquiries (contact form + WhatsApp).

## Target users
Affluent travelers (couples, families, small groups) planning a high-touch Nosara trip. They are comparing villas and destinations and want a trusted local operator, not a booking engine.

## Brand personality
Direct, specific, slightly contrarian. Anti-template, anti-luxury-cliche. Warm but not soft. Specificity beats polish: a place, a price, a person, a number over an adjective. Editorial voice rules: no em dashes, no AI-talk vocabulary, no made-up facts, no naming third-party competitors (Monkey Head Adventures excepted).

## Anti-references
Generic resort sites, OTA booking funnels, AI-generated luxury-travel landing pages. The "we curate seamless experiences" voice. Stock-photo gloss with no real local detail.

## Strategic design principles
1. Image-led. Real Nosara/villa/boat photography carries the design; never colored placeholder blocks.
2. Every page is built for SEO and AI-crawl: JSON-LD schema, semantic HTML, descriptive alt text and filenames.
3. Trust through specificity. Real names, counts, and local knowledge over marketing adjectives.
4. Calm, warm, coastal-jungle palette and serif/sans pairing already shipped; identity is committed, preserve it.
5. Convert without pressure: soft inquiry CTAs (sticky bar, contact, WhatsApp), invite repeat trips.

## Stack
Astro 4 static site, deployed on Cloudflare Workers Static Assets. Tokens in `src/styles/global.css`. Fonts: Playfair Display (display) + DM Sans (body).
